About GoKifuStudio

Language and Views

Use 日本語 / English beside Simple View to change the interface language. In Simple View, language selection is in View. Your choice is remembered. Switching languages does not alter game records, comments, player names, engine profiles or ongoing analysis.

Normal View has board controls on the left and detailed panels on the right. Hide Panel makes more room for the board. Drag a panel boundary to resize it; double-click to restore the standard width. Simple View shows a large board with a compact menu and move slider. Detail panels open in separate movable, resizable windows.

Windows remembers window position, size and maximized state separately for Normal and Simple View. The board stays square and fits the available space. Very tall, narrow windows can leave unused space below it.

Editing and Navigation

  • Lock Record prevents accidental edits while allowing navigation and analysis. Unlock it to play or edit stones and marks.
  • Choose board sizes from 4 to 19. Handicap setup is available in the board controls and game settings.
  • Playing an alternative move creates a variation. Insert Here adds moves inside a line. Variations can be deleted or promoted to the main line.
  • Click a stone to go to its move. Use the red slider, Go to Move, arrow keys or mouse wheel to navigate. Auto replays the selected line. Starting at its end shows a warning.
  • Move Numbers and Last control which stone numbers appear, including exported images and printouts. The recent-move choices include a custom count. This is not automatic pagination of a whole game.
  • Switch Turn changes the next player. Rotation and mirror operations also affect exported coordinates. Swap Colors exchanges colors and related game information. Undo is available.
  • Use Moves / Tools to place setup stones, marks, labels or text. Items marked ＊ support dragging. Clear Board clears the record and resets tools, playback and analysis; Undo restores the record but does not restart stopped tasks.

Opening and Saving

Supported imports are SGF, NGF, UGF, UGI, GIB and MGT. Encoding is normally detected automatically. Open a file, drop it on the board, or paste SGF with Ctrl+V. Save Entire Record includes variations and comments. Main-line-only and comment-free saves are also available. Records without moves, setup stones or marks cannot be saved.

Export board images as PNG or JPG, create animated GIFs of a move range, or print the current board. Black & White Image redraws at least twice the displayed size and at least 1600 pixels. The displayed board is not recolored. Native save dialogs use the executable folder as their initial location and select the filename separately from the extension.

Local Backup stores one overwriteable copy on this device. Auto Backup saves after editing settles. It survives closing the app but is not uploaded or shared. Save important records as SGF files.

KataGo Analysis

Engines lets you register the executable, model, optional GTP config and additional arguments. Keep required DLLs beside the executable. Engine profiles can be reordered by dragging or arrow buttons. The bundled engine is KataGo-Eigen. Other KataGo builds and models may be selected. Analysis and play are KataGo-only.

Start Analysis or Space toggles continuous analysis of the current position. It follows moves and variation changes. Hover over a candidate to preview its principal variation (PV); click to play that candidate when unlocked, or use F2 for the top move. PV length only limits the displayed continuation, not search depth.

Blue is the top candidate. Green, yellow and orange indicate increasing score loss relative to it. A gold outline identifies the next played move; a red mark identifies the last move. Low-visit evaluations can change substantially. By default, candidate values use the side-to-play perspective.

Full Game Analysis processes the selected line with a time limit per position; the default is 0.1 seconds for a quick review. Analysis can be saved with the record and reopened without an engine. Loss Ranking is separated by color and can be sorted by win rate or points. Each list initially shows ten entries; expand it to see the rest.

Regions, Graphs and Estimates

Region Only restricts candidate and PV moves to marked intersections; passing remains possible. Stones outside the region stay on the board and the evaluation is still for the whole game. Include the relevant stones and surrounding empty intersections. This is not a guaranteed life-and-death solver.

Drag the Region tool to enclose an area, then right-click inside to fill it, including occupied intersections. The board edge can form part of the boundary. Right-clicking the other side fills that side. Undo restores the previous region.

The evaluation bar shows black and white win rates. The graph labels identify the perspective. Ordinary analysis uses black win rate and black score lead. In AI–AI games, black and white lines show each AI's own win rate; score estimates retain each AI's own perspective. Missing positions are left blank. Click the graph to navigate when a game is not actively running.

Estimate uses the analysis engine to estimate ownership and score, with about two seconds of search excluding startup. During any game, it first pauses play and stops the game engines. Resume explicitly when finished. The estimate is not final scoring or guaranteed life-and-death judgment.

Score supports Japanese and Chinese rules. Use it after boundaries are settled and necessary moves have been played. Mark dead stones and seki manually. It is available for loaded records and after a game ends by two passes; it is not enabled during play.

Playing Games

Select Human or a registered KataGo for each side. Human–human, human–AI and AI–AI are supported. Start Game asks whether to clear an existing position or continue from it. Set the game type, handicap, komi and players before starting.

AI limits are per move: Unlimited, 0.5, 1, 2, 5, 10 seconds or Custom. Visits default to unlimited; either limit ends the search when reached. These settings override CFG search limits. Both limits unlimited can leave the engine thinking indefinitely until paused. Startup and synchronization are not included. Human limits are per turn; timeout loses the game.

AI resignation is enabled by default. For even games the default threshold is 3% over three consecutive own turns, passed to KataGo. Handicap games use Allow / Disallow, with KataGo's own handicap safeguards. The early-resignation prohibition defaults to zero and does not disable internal safeguards. Pondering on the opponent's turn is disabled.

Pause stops the game engines. Resume restarts them at the same position; changing the position requires a new game. Take Back undoes the last human move and its replies, or one move in a human–human game. It is unavailable in AI–AI games and does not refund time already spent. Ending or resigning pauses the game before confirmation.

Two passes end the game. Resignation and timeout results are written to SGF. Save Game saves the actual played line with player names, game name and date. Use the regular save menu to retain all variations. During-game loss rankings are provisional and may compare different engines; use full game analysis afterward for a consistent review.

Image Import

Choose, drop or paste a photo or board diagram. Mark the visible board area and its four corner intersections. Recognize Stones finds a tentative position; click intersections to correct it and check the black/white counts before importing.

Set handicap, komi, next player and captured stones. Handicap defaults to zero. A full 19×19 board defaults to komi 6.5; partial diagrams and other sizes default to zero. Setting handicap to 1–9 selects White and komi 0.5, which you can override. Captured black stones are White's prisoners, and vice versa. Initial capture counts use custom SGF properties that other software may ignore.

Move order and ko history cannot be recovered from one image. Areas outside a partial diagram are unknown. Always check the recognized position.

Keyboard Shortcuts

  • Space: start / stop analysis; F2: play top candidate.
  • Home / End: first / final position in the current line.
  • ↓ / ↑: next / previous move; Shift+↓ / Shift+↑: ten moves.
  • Mouse wheel down / up over the board: next / previous move.
  • M: move numbers; C: coordinates; O: open a game.
  • Del: delete the current move and continuations. Ctrl+Home: clear the board after confirmation.
  • Ctrl+V: paste a game, or paste an image when using image import.

Text input and dialogs take priority over game shortcuts. This version is for Windows PCs; mobile/tablet development is deferred.

Appearance & Sound

Open the footer control or View → Appearance & Sound in Simple View. The independent window has Text, Board/Background, Graph and Sound tabs. Changes preview immediately. Apply saves, while Cancel or closing restores saved settings. Reset Defaults also requires Apply.

Text settings

Body text is 12/14/16/18px, prominent text 14/16/18/20px, help text 12/12/14/16px. Japanese and English remember sizes and font families separately. Choose or type an installed font name; unavailable fonts fall back. Board labels scale 80/100/120%. Large text may wrap or require scrolling.

Themes and audio

Choose colors, board texture and stone style, and graph colors/line width. Images must be PNG/JPEG/WebP up to 8 MB, resized to at most 512px for storage. WAV files must be up to 1 MB. Volume zero is silent. Played moves, navigation and autoplay are separate options; PV hover is silent. The three bundled synthesized sounds are original and do not use LizzieYzy assets.
